Read the Link Before You Follow It

Know the Role, Not the Reputation

Before you click, recognize what the link is for. A link's role is about navigation, not credibility.

✓What a link role tells you

  • Where the link sits in the path.
  • Whether it organizes, references, routes, or arrives.
  • What it is for at this step.

✕What a link role does not tell you

  • That the link is safe, verified, live, or working.
  • That the destination is trustworthy or current.
  • That every link is maintained by the same party.

Every Destination Can Change

Know When to Stop and Re-check

  • 1

    Title has changed. The destination no longer matches the row. Stop and re-read the page.

  • 2

    Options differ. Color, size, or version is not as recorded. Stop and record the difference.

  • 3

    Images differ. What the page shows is not what the row referenced. Stop and recheck.

  • 4

    Stock, price, or policy changed. Availability, price context, or terms differ. Stop and confirm before relying on it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

A HipoBuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a reference to research, not an official, live, or complete product catalog.

Is this website the official HipoBuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by HipoBuy or Google.

What are the four link roles?

Spreadsheet organizes the public entry and overall browsing scope. Row reference provides one row's local clue and context. Category route helps narrow the browsing category. Next destination shows the information actually visible on the current page. These roles describe navigation purpose, not credibility, quality, or live status.

Does this site verify links or report link status?

No. This resource is not a link validator, a URL converter, an order tracker, or a QC service. It does not test links, report live status, or label anything safe, verified, live, or working.

How do I read a row reference?

Read a row in order: the visible label, the selected option, the source context, and the details that still need re-checking. A row is a starting point, not a completed check.

What must I re-check at the destination?

Title, options, images, stock, price, and policy can all change after you leave the current page, so re-confirm them on the destination page you actually open.
